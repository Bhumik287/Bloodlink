Educational Project & Clinical Disclaimer

BloodLink is a student college project designed for communication coordination. It does not medically verify donor suitability or replace hospital blood banks. Actual donation, cross-matching, and compatibility testing must be conducted by certified hospital medical professionals.

How It Works

Three-step emergency coordination workflow.

1
Create a Request

The patient or relative enters the required blood group, units, hospital name, and urgency level.

2
Donors Check & Respond

Available donors browse open requests filtered by blood group and locality, and click "I'm Available to Help".

3
Coordinate Directly

The requester receives the donor's contact details in their dashboard and calls them to coordinate donation at the hospital.
